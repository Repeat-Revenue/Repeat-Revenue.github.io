Privacy notice

This explains what happens to the information you share through this website.

Who I am

Repeat Revenue is the trading name of Rapheal Chukwuka, an independent email marketing specialist. I'm responsible for the personal information you share here. You can contact me at rapheal.i.chukwuka@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Audit form: your name, business name, email, website, email platform, list size, goal and any message you write.
  • Mailing list: your first name and email address, if you join my list.

This website doesn't use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies.

How I use it

  • To prepare your audit, reply to you and quote for work you ask about.
  • If you join my list: to send you my welcome emails and occasional emails about email marketing. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

I won't add audit requests to my mailing list without your permission.

Lawful basis

For audit requests, I rely on legitimate interests in replying to a request you've made, and on taking steps at your request before a contract. For the mailing list, I rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing.

Who else handles it

  • Web3Forms delivers audit form submissions to my inbox.
  • Brevo stores my mailing list and sends its emails.
  • Google (Gmail) is where I receive and store emails.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on this site, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

I don't sell your information or share it with anyone else.

International transfers

I'm based in Nigeria, and the providers above may process data in other countries. I keep your information secure by using reputable providers and limiting access to myself.

How long I keep it

Audit requests that don't lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Mailing list details are kept until you unsubscribe. Client records are kept as long as needed for the work and for legal or accounting obligations.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how I use it, at any time by emailing rapheal.i.chukwuka@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live.
