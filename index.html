An everyday story

Amara found a little candle shop online.

She bought one candle. She loved it. She even told a friend about it.

Then… nothing. No "how's the candle?" No "here's what's new." Not a single email.

Three months later, an ad from a different shop caught her eye. She bought from them instead.

The candle shop never knew they'd lost her. They just wondered why sales felt flat.

That's the quiet leak in most businesses. Not a lack of new customers, but a lack of reasons for old ones to come back.

Email is how you give them that reason. That's what I do.

Where the money goes

Three leaks quietly draining your sales.

None of them are obvious. All of them are fixable with emails that send themselves.

i.

No welcome email

Someone joins your list. They're more interested in you right now than they'll ever be again. If nothing arrives, that interest fades within days.

What it costs: the easiest sale you'll ever make.

ii.

Carts left behind

People add to basket, then the phone rings or the kettle boils. Without a friendly reminder, most of them never come back to finish.

What it costs: buyers who had already decided.

iii.

Silence after the sale

No thank-you, no tips, no "you might also like". Your customer forgets you, and the next brand that emails them wins.

What it costs: repeat business you already earned.

Questions

Fair questions.

Which email platforms do you work with?

Mainly Klaviyo, Mailchimp and Brevo. If you don't have one yet, I'll recommend the right fit for your business and set it up for you.

Do I need a big email list?

No. A small list of people who have already bought from you is worth more than a big list of strangers. And if your list is tiny, growing it is part of the work.

Will the emails sound like me?

Yes. I study your website, your social pages and what your customers say about you, then write in your voice. Nothing goes live until you've approved it.

Will this get me marked as spam?

I only email people who chose to hear from you, include a clear unsubscribe link in every email and follow good sending practice, so your emails keep landing in the inbox.

Where are you based?

Lagos, Nigeria. I work remotely across time zones and plan calls around your working day. You always deal with me directly.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need, so I quote after the free audit. You'll get a clear written quote before any work starts, with no long contracts.
